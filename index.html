From Training Programs to Workforce Community

Skill It Up™ began as a workforce initiative designed and delivered by Knowledge Transfer Consulting Services (KTCS), supported through U.S. Department of Labor (DOL) funded programs.

Through delivering pre-apprenticeship programs and connecting participants with employers, we learned that workforce development does not end at training— it continues through connection, mentorship, and long-term opportunity.

Today, Skill It Up is evolving into a connected learning community that brings together: learners, alumni, employers, and industry professionals.

Ongoing Learning

Continuous access to certifications, skills, and pathways

Employer Engagement

Direct connection between talent and hiring partners

Mentorship

Guidance from instructors and industry professionals

Career Growth

Long-term pathways beyond initial training programs

As the Skill It Up platform grows, expanded access and community features will be available to future participants and partners.
